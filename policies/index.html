Refund policy

All sales are final. Because you get instant access to digital video workshops and downloadable guides, we do not offer refunds once a purchase is complete.

Please look through the course details, the sample projects and the FAQ before you buy. If you are unsure which pack suits your child, start with the Starter Pack and upgrade later, or message us first.

When we will help

  • Charged twice by mistake. We refund the duplicate payment in full.
  • You cannot get into the course. Write to us and we will fix your access. If we cannot give you working access within 7 days of your message, we refund what you paid.

To ask for either, email kokoromind.info@gmail.com within 7 days of payment, with the name, email and phone number used at checkout. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 7 to 10 working days, depending on your bank.

Not refundable

  • A change of mind, or not finding time to watch.
  • Not having the craft materials. They are everyday items listed in each workshop.
  • Access that has ended after the access period you bought (6 or 12 months).

Privacy policy

On this website

This website does not ask for your name or contact details and does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To show the right prices, it reads your device's time zone inside your own browser; nothing is sent to us. Your browser remembers two choices on your device: the price region and whether the flying butterflies are on.

When you buy

Payments and course access are handled by SuperProfile. When you enroll, SuperProfile collects the details needed for payment and to send your access links, such as your name, email and phone number, and shares them with us so we can support you. We never see your card or bank details. SuperProfile's own privacy policy also applies.

How we use your details

  • To give you access, answer your questions and tell you about your course.
  • We do not sell or rent your details to anyone.
  • We keep them only as long as needed for your course and for our accounts.

WhatsApp, Instagram and email

If you join our WhatsApp community, other members can see your name and phone number, as in any WhatsApp group. Please do not share your child's personal details, or photos showing their face, unless you are comfortable with other parents seeing them. WhatsApp and Instagram have their own privacy policies.

Children

The courses are for children aged 4 to 10, bought and managed by a parent or guardian. We do not collect information directly from children.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, email kokoromind.info@gmail.com.

Safety

Some activities use scissors, paints, glue and small items. An adult should supervise, especially for children under 6. Use child-safe, non-toxic materials, and keep small parts away from young children.

Results

The workshops support creativity, focus and fine motor skills through play. Every child is different, so we cannot promise particular developmental results.
